[2] Relevant Limits from <climits> and <limits>:
  - int32_t Min / Max : -2147483648 to 2147483647
  - int64_t Min / Max : -9223372036854775808 to 9223372036854775807
  - double Digits10   : 15 decimal digits precision
  - float Digits10    : 6 decimal digits precision

[3] Reproducible Precision Comparison: Floating-Point vs Integer Paise:
  - Adding Rs 0.10 repeatedly 100,000 times:
    * Expected Total         : Rs 10000.0000000000
    * Floating-Point (double): Rs 10000.0000000188 (Error: 0.0000000188)
    * Integer Paise (int64)  : Rs 10000.0000000000 (Error: 0.0000000000 EXACT)

[4] Integer Overflow Demonstration in Fare Accumulation:
  - Accumulating 3,000,000 journeys @ 2500 paise:
    * Expected Total (Mathematical) : 7,500,000,000 paise (Rs 75,000,000.00)
    * int32_t Accumulator (32-bit)  : -1089934592 paise (OVERFLOW/WRAP-AROUND!)
    * int64_t Accumulator (64-bit)  : 7500000000 paise (CORRECT & STABLE)

[5] Complexity & Design Trade-off Comparison:
  +-------------------+------------+-------------+--------------------------------------+

  | double (rupees)   | O(1)       | O(1) 8B     | Accumulated rounding error           |
