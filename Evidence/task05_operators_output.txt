[3] Prefix vs Postfix Increment Semantics:
  - Initial: val = 10
  - After ++val : pre = 11, val = 11
  - After val++ : post = 11, val = 12

[4] Analysis of 'i = i++' Undefined/Unspecified Behavior:
  - In C++03, modifying 'i' more than once between sequence points causes UNDEFINED BEHAVIOR.
  - In C++17 (P0145R3), assignment evaluates right side before left side, but
    writing 'i = i++' produces conflicting write intents, confusing compilers and human readers.
  - It must strictly be replaced with simple '++i;' or 'i += 1;'.
